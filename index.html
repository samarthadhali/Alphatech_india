HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Will this tracker work with my bike or car?

Compatibility depends on the exact tracker model, power requirements, vehicle wiring and installation method. Share your vehicle make, model and year so the team can check before purchase.

Is the mobile app or platform included in the device price?

Package inclusions and recurring platform or connectivity charges should be confirmed in the quotation. Ask for the subscription duration, renewal price and what happens if a plan expires.

Does it support ignition alerts, overspeed alerts and geofencing?

These features depend on the supplied device and software configuration. Ask the ALPHATECH team to confirm which features are available for your selected model.

How much does installation cost?

Installation cost can depend on vehicle type and location. Request a quotation that separately lists device, installation, platform and any connectivity charges.

What warranty do I get?

Please request the current written warranty terms, exclusions, claim process and service contact before ordering. Do not rely on an unspecified warranty period.

Can I buy for a fleet or become a dealer?

Use the enquiry form and select “Dealer / bulk enquiry” to discuss quantity, supported vehicles, commercial terms and service arrangements.

Want to see how it fits your vehicle?

Ask for a product demo, current pricing and compatibility confirmation.
